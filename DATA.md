# Data and attribution

The MIT licence in `LICENSE` covers the engine code: JavaScript, CSS, HTML templates, JSON Schema, and build scripts.

It does not relicense regional datasets. Those datasets are published by each edition, not by this repository, and they follow the licence of every source they are derived from. An edition may show a record on the public map only when `publication_status` is `public_approved`, which requires the source, the terms of use, the geometry, and the limits of interpretation to have been checked. A missing value is not a zero.

Map tiles stay under their providers. The atlas credits OpenStreetMap contributors and Esri imagery in the map control.

Illustrations in `shared/outreach-assets/` are copied into an edition site by the build for the outreach modules. `brotzu-portrait.gif` is a historical portrait used in the Brotzu module. This licence does not claim to extinguish any rights that remain in the underlying photograph.

Linked pages (WHO, ECDC, ISS, EFSA, Treccani, and the scientific literature) stay under their own publishers. Title links are citations, not a republication of those works.
